# Ownership and rights-chain status

**Status:** project-owner decision recorded; private rights-chain review open
**Last updated:** 2026-07-30

The authorized public role for repository licensing decisions is
`ZUGFOLGE_PROJECT_OWNER`. The public decision evidence is referenced and hashed
in [`LICENSE-DECISION.md`](LICENSE-DECISION.md) and the machine-readable
[`rights-register.yaml`](../../compliance/rights-register.yaml).

Repository history and GitHub ownership are useful audit inputs, but they are
not treated as complete proof of copyright, employment, contractor, asset, or
trademark ownership. Private identity documents, contracts, and legal opinions
must not be committed.

Before MS-001 can close, an accountable human review must map every material
historic contribution and asset to one of these outcomes:

- covered by the authorized rights holder;
- covered by a compatible third-party license with retained notices;
- not independently protectable but safe to distribute;
- replaced or removed because the rights chain is insufficient.

External contributions have not been opened. The selected future CLA model is
not active until qualified terms and enforcement exist.
