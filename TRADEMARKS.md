# Zugfolge trademarks and brand assets

**Status:** provisional; clearance and ownership evidence pending
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-30

`Zugfolge`, the Block-Z device, and the claim
`Plane präzise. Fahre profitabel.` are intended project identifiers. This file
does not claim that any of them is registered, legally cleared, or exclusively
owned by an identified rights holder.

No software, documentation, or data license in this repository grants
permission to use these identifiers in a way that implies endorsement, an
official game world, or affiliation with Zugfolge. Descriptive references,
truthful compatibility statements, repository forks, and notices required by
an applicable license remain unaffected. Forks should use a distinct name and
logo unless the future rights holder grants written permission.

Before public brand use, humans must separately:

- identify the trademark and domain owner;
- document the Block-Z creation tools, references, source files, human edits,
  AI assistance, and rights transfer;
- search `Zugfolge`, Block-Z, and the claim for identical and similar marks in
  relevant classes and territories;
- assess the word's railway-technical meaning and resulting distinctiveness;
- acquire and privately evidence control of the intended domain;
- choose a fallback name/mark and renewal owner;
- obtain qualified trademark review.

DPMAregister, EUIPO/TMview, WIPO, web, and DENIC searches provide evidence, not
a legal guarantee or reservation:

- [DPMAregister](https://register.dpma.de/DPMAregister/marke/einsteiger)
- [EUIPO/TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/)
- [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/)
- [DENIC RDAP](https://www.denic.de/services/whois-service/rdap-service/)

The project must not use `®` unless a corresponding registration actually
exists. Current state and next actions are recorded in
[`compliance/rights-register.yaml`](compliance/rights-register.yaml).
